Product overview

DRIME is a responsive, multilingual, interactive, embeddable video player with no cookies, plus an integrated video editor. It relies on a custom .drime JSON-based format that enables playback in multiple languages (subtitles, voiceovers, dynamic designs) from a single video file. This reduces production time, storage costs, and the ecological footprint associated with file storage (one hosted video per project, one DRIME file per project).

1/ DRIME Player & .drime file format

For professionals/clients

- Video tutorials are filmed by the DRIME team and optimized for all screen resolutions.
- Features include multi-language broadcasting via voiceovers, subtitles, and dynamic designs in all selected languages, plus voice commands. Tutorials/manuals can be broken into step-by-step sequences.
- Beyond the DRIME platform’s video library, clients can embed single videos or entire libraries on their own sites via iframes (indexed by Google).
- The platform includes a dashboard with “Smart Indicators”, user-rights management, custom iframes, and QR codes. Dynamic video designs were also road-mapped.

For end users

- A video playback experience that adapts to screen resolution, with step-by-step or continuous playback, voice commands, sound/subtitle settings, and language selection.
- By default, the video displays in the user’s browser language when available, otherwise in English.

2/ DRIME Video Editor

What it is
A standalone editor (distinct from the player) to create slideshows from short videos/photos with customizable transitions. Like the player, it’s multilingual, responsive, interactive, embeddable, and lightweight.

Speed of production
With pre-selected media and text, clients can create & publish a video in ~5 minutes. Adding another language (voiceover, subtitles, etc.) takes ~2 minutes. Logos and soundtracks can be added. In short, with minimal prep, a multilingual video can be produced in ~10 minutes and published immediately. Export via iframe, a single embed code line, or as a showcase on the DRIME platform
